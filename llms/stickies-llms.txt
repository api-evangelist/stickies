# Stickies

> Stickies (stickies.wtf) was a Los Angeles-based consumer web3 product by BYO Labs that made "dumb stickers and GIFs starring your favorite NFTs" — users connected a crypto wallet and generated stickers/GIFs of their NFT avatars. Backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners (~$5.5M raised); founded circa 2022 by Tom Wright and Sterling Barnett. No public developer API, documentation, or SDK was ever published. The company appears defunct: stickies.wtf was last live in 2023 and the domain no longer resolves (NXDOMAIN as of 2026-07).
